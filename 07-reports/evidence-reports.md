## Evidence Types

### Required Evidence by Activity

| Activity | Evidence Required | Format |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| Field Visit | Activity report, photos, attendance | PDF, JPG |
| Training | Attendance, photos, evaluation | PDF, JPG |
| Survey | Completed forms, data sheet | PDF, Excel |
| Meeting | Minutes, attendance, action items | PDF |
| Purchase | Receipts, delivery note | PDF, JPG |
